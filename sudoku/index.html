About this app

Enjoy the ultimate Sudoku experience with Pure Sudoku, a classic number puzzle game designed to improve your logical thinking, concentration, memory, and problem-solving skills. Whether you're a beginner learning Sudoku or an expert seeking the toughest challenges, Pure Sudoku offers a smooth, engaging, and rewarding puzzle-solving journey.

Progress through hundreds of handcrafted puzzles, earn XP, level up, and unlock advanced challenges where only one mistake is allowed!

💯 500+ Handcrafted Levels

Carefully designed levels that gradually increase in difficulty, plus 100 extra premium levels for ultimate masters! Includes an infinite Random Puzzle Generator.

🧠 4 Difficulty Modes

Choose from Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert. Plus daily Sudoku challenges to maintain your solving streak.

🧩 Smart Sudoku Solver

Stuck on a newspaper puzzle? Enter your own puzzle manually and instantly solve it or analyze difficult boards.

✏️ Smart Notes & Hints

Auto-fill and auto-clear notes as you solve the grid. Receive guidance without revealing the entire solution.

🏆 Level Progression

Earn XP by solving puzzles. After Level 300, the challenge intensifies: only 1 mistake allowed!
