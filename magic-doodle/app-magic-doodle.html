About this app

Welcome to Magic Doodle: Kids Drawing App – the ultimate creative canvas, paint studio, and coloring book designed specifically for children, toddlers, and young artists! With our easy-to-use kids paint tools, vibrant color palettes, and engaging coloring pages, your child will spend hours exploring their imagination, improving their fine motor skills, and creating beautiful doodle art.

Magic Doodle is packed with a fantastic coloring book for kids featuring cute animals, magical creatures, cars, princesses, and more. 100% compliant with COPPA and Google Play's Families policies.

🎨 Free Drawing Mode

Blank canvas allows kids to draw, sketch, and paint whatever they dream up.

🖌️ Magic Brushes & Tools

Choose from crayons, markers, glowing neon magic brushes, water colors, and paint rollers.

🖍️ Fun Coloring Book

Tap to fill areas or use advanced brush modes to stay in the lines like a real coloring book.

📸 Draw on Photos

Import a picture and draw funny hats, mustaches, or magical backgrounds on family photos.

🌟 Safe & Offline

Child-safe ads, strictly filtered. Play entirely offline without a connection.
