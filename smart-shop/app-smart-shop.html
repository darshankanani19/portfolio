About this app

Simplify your business management with Smart Shop Manager, the all-in-one POS and inventory solution designed for modern shop owners. Whether you run a retail store, a small grocery, or a hardware shop, our app helps you digitise your records and streamline operations.

Why Choose Smart Shop Manager?
Smart Shop Manager is built to be fast, secure, and user-friendly. Say goodbye to manual ledger books and welcome a digital, data-driven way to grow your business. Download Smart Shop Manager today and take total control of your business!

📦 Inventory Management

Keep track of your stock levels in real-time. Add, update, and manage products with ease.

💰 Quick Billing & POS

Generate professional digital bills for your customers in seconds.

👥 Staff Management

Add staff members to your shop and collaborate securely with role-based access.

📊 Detailed Reports

Get insights into your best-selling products and monthly revenue with clear charts and PDF exports.

☁️ Secure Cloud Sync & Offline

Powered by Supabase, data is securely synced. Works offline to record entries locally.
