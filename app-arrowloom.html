About this app

Get ready to untangle your mind and test your logic with Arrowloom, the most addictive and relaxing 3D block puzzle game! Your goal is simple: tap the blocks to make them fly away and clear the board. But there’s a catch—the blocks will only fly in the direction their arrows are pointing!

Swipe to rotate the massive 3D shapes, find the perfect angle, and strategize your moves to solve intricate, brain-teasing puzzles. As you progress, the shapes get bigger, the blocks get trickier, and the puzzles get incredibly rewarding!

🧩 Satisfying 3D Gameplay

Tap, rotate, and watch the blocks satisfyingly fly away off the screen!

🧠 Brain-Training Puzzles

Challenge your logic, critical thinking, and spatial awareness with hundreds of unique, handcrafted levels.

🔄 Rotate & Solve

Swipe to spin the 3D shapes a full 360 degrees. Find the hidden blocks and clear a path!

🏆 Exciting Daily Challenges

Complete specific tasks and milestones to earn massive coin rewards in our brand-new Challenges system!

🎡 Spin the Wheel

Test your luck every day with the Daily Spin to win free hints, extra lives, and bonus coins!
