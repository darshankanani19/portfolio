Privacy Policy for Magic Doodle Kids

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Magic Doodle Kids. We respect your privacy and the privacy of children. We are committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

2. Data We Collect

As an application designed for children, we do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from our users.

  • Camera/Photo Library: We may request access to save drawings locally on your device. All images remain on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted to our servers.
  • Device Data: We collect anonymized usage data for the internal operations of the app.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To allow users to draw and save pictures on their device.
  • To improve the app performance and fix bugs through anonymous analytics (Firebase).

4. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. These ads are strictly contextual and child-directed. We do not use behavioral tracking, and we have disabled personalized ads in compliance with COPPA.
